I'm a Senior Product Manager in Frankfurt am Main, and I work on payment products for public transport 
💳 You tap your bank card at the gate, and I look after what happens between that tap and the money settling: EMV acceptance at the validator, the account-based back office, and the banks and PSPs in between.

**Senior Product Manager, Cubic Transportation Systems** (2026–present, at Cubic since 2021) · 📍 Rhein-Main · 🗣️ German and English

Today that's Umo Pass, open-loop payments for transit in the US, Canada and Oceania. Umo is not in EMEA yet, so I also do the compliance groundwork for its first European bids, where EU digital identity at the gate meets account-based ticketing and EPI. I've spent 15+ years in payments and regulated work, starting at PayPal in Dublin.

## Some of the work I got to be part of

- 📱 **A transit app with 3M+ users went from 1.6 to 4.0 stars** through the Deutschland-Ticket launch, with zero downtime. The client renewed at €2M+ a year. I was the Senior Technical Project Manager on that €5M+ B2G program and led 20+ people across vendors and partner organizations, with nobody reporting to me. [How the delivery was run →](case-studies/deutschland-ticket-turnaround.md)
- 💳 **Open-loop payments from the tap to settlement**, which is my job today. [Case study →](case-studies/open-loop-payments-end-to-end.md)
- 🏦 **A banking act turned into engineering specs.** At Velvon Bank I turned KWG requirements into specs for core banking on GCP, for a banking-license acquisition under BaFin scrutiny. The parent stopped the German launch in early 2020, before the bank opened. [Case study →](case-studies/banking-act-to-specifications.md)
- 🤖 **An AI usage standard that is in use.** I wrote my product org's AI usage standard on one idea: AI drafts and sorts, and a named person signs off on anything a regulator or a passenger sees. The EU AI Act calls that human oversight (Article 14). I practise the same rule on a small infrastructure lab at home 🧪 [Case study →](case-studies/hub-and-spoke-estate.md)

None of it was solo work. The teams built it.

## What I work with

- 💳 **Payments:** open-loop EMV, Instant Payments, EPI/Wero, PSD2/PSD3/PSR, account-based ticketing
- 🤖 **AI governance:** EU AI Act, ISO/IEC 42001
- 🛡️ **Regulation:** DORA, MaRisk/BAIT, BaFin, KWG, MiFID II, and one rank down KRITIS/NIS2

I have delivered under KWG and BaFin supervision, a Tier-1 bank's MiFID II programme (PMO), gaming-license and payment compliance, PSD2 and public-sector procurement. DORA, KRITIS/NIS2, the EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001 are advisory and bid work so far.
