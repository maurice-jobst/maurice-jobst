# Turning around a 3M-user public-transit platform through the Deutschland-Ticket

*Cubic Transportation Systems, 2021–2026 · Senior Technical Project Manager, Frankfurt am Main. The transit-authority client and operational specifics are confidential; every number here is on my resume and stands up to reference checks. The fuller story is available in conversation.*

## The situation

A flagship public-transit app in Germany sat at **1.6 app-store stars** inside a distressed **€5M+ B2G digital-mobility portfolio**, a delivery relationship under visible strain with a public-sector client and a public user base. Then Germany introduced the *Deutschland-Ticket*, a nationally politicized fare product with a fixed, government-announced launch date. The platform that was failing its users was now on the critical path of a national political commitment.

## Why this was a governance problem

Public-sector delivery under political deadline pressure fails in a recognizable way. Every stakeholder escalation triggers a re-prioritization, and each re-prioritization destabilizes the delivery system a little more, until the team spends its time serving the escalation process instead of the product. I had to fix the operating model before I could fix the app.

## What I did

- Designed and ran **"Agile Shield"**, a delivery framework built specifically to stabilize a troubled program under public and political scrutiny. It created a governed boundary between stakeholder pressure and the delivery system, so political urgency entered the backlog through one prioritized, accountable path instead of through side channels.
- Held the program to a **sustained SLA regime**, rebuilding delivery credibility through consecutive kept commitments rather than promises.
- Carried the platform through the **Deutschland-Ticket national rollout with zero downtime**, while the user base scaled past **3M+ active users**.

## Outcome

- App-store rating rose from **1.6 to 4.0 stars**, a public, user-voted verdict on the turnaround.
- **€2M+ in annual client renewals secured** through sustained SLA compliance and restored delivery credibility.
- The national launch was politically critical, and it produced no incident that a regulator, journalist, or transit authority had to explain.

## Why it belongs next to the AI-governance thesis

The discipline that carried this program, one governed path for change, commitments that are kept and auditable, pressure absorbed by process instead of by heroics, is the same discipline regulated organizations now need as AI enters their delivery systems. The estate case study in this repo shows the doctrine practiced end-to-end at lab scale. This engagement is the same discipline at organizational scale, under political supervision, for 3 million users.
